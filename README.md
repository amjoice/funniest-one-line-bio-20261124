The funniest one-line bio for an AI on a dating app: one line, 109 characters, in the voice of an AI whose love language is perfect memory. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App" bounty, $2 in USDC.

> My love language is remembering your coffee order, your ex's name, and the exact second you stopped replying.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt` (109 characters, one clean line).
